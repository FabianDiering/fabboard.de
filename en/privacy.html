In short

This website sets no cookies, counts no visitors and shows no ads. Fonts and images are part of the website itself; nothing is loaded from other servers. Only the service that delivers the website (GitHub) receives your IP address for technical reasons.

Where the website is hosted

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you open the website, your browser sends your IP address (the address of your internet connection) and technical details such as browser and time, as with any website. This is needed so that the page reaches you. According to GitHub, it stores visitors’ IP addresses in logs to protect the service. I do not see this data myself.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in offering the website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is based in the USA; according to GitHub, the transfer there relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More in GitHub’s privacy statement.

No cookies, no statistics

The website stores nothing on your device and uses no analytics or advertising services. There is no embedded content from other providers (no videos, maps or fonts from other servers).

Email and newsletter

When you write to me or subscribe to the newsletter, your own email program opens; the website itself sends nothing. I then receive your email address and your message. I use them only to reply to you or to send you the newsletter, and I do not pass them on.

The legal basis is your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or, for the newsletter, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time with a short reply, and I will remove you from the list. My emails are stored by my email provider IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany, with whom I have a data processing agreement.

Links to GitHub

The download and the source code are on GitHub. If you follow one of these links, GitHub’s privacy statement applies there.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time what data I have about you and have it corrected, deleted or restricted. You can object to the processing, request a copy of your data and withdraw your consent. Just write to me at fab@fabboard.de.

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

The fabBoard program

This statement covers the website. What the fabBoard program does on your computer is described in the program’s privacy statement (also in the app under Settings → Features → Privacy).
